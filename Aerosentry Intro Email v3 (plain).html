Asymmetric-warfare overmatch

Defeat massed drone attack at the cost of a magazine, not a missile.

Aerosentry builds intelligent, recoverable, scalable interception systems. The interceptors, flown by agentic AI, neutralise swarm attacks autonomously and deploy in minutes across any environment.
The challenge

Massed one-way-attacks saturate air defences, degrade combat power and exhaust limited personnel and resources. The exchange ratio favours the attacker: cheap drones traded against expensive interceptors.

  
Our system

A recoverable, modular-payload interceptor reverses that ratio: rapid multi-domain deployment, agentic AI piloting for autonomous threat neutralisation, and additive manufacturing that scales with the threat.
